# Changelog

## 0.1.6-beta — 2026-09-14

Pre-release. The next version is 1.0.

The stool hath learned to wander. There is no grid, nor any four-way prison: hold left, hold right, and the lead end turns upon the whole compass. The body follows as a chain of lumps, lit in three dimensions, cracked and clumped as nature would have it — not a cartoon serpent with eyes, but a piece of poo that has somewhere to be.

The cabinet is landscape now, a handheld built for a laptop’s horizon, screen in the middle, L and R in the wings. The floor is earth, not a checkerboard. Stains smear and fade. Steam still rises.

The farts are recordings, not the beep a 1985 computer would swear was flatulence. Under them, an original theme with a beginning, a middle, and a little soul, which knows when to duck.

## 0.1.3

A flash of *YOU SUCK* was promised upon the glass. The glass was unmoved. Preserved here as a noble failure, not as a joke at the player’s expense so much as at our own.

## 0.1.2

Steam rose from the coil. The brown body was dressed for the stage. The cabinet found a voice: a thin looping air, and a family of synthesized farts — which sounded, in hindsight, like what a machine might invent if it had never met a body. We kept the build. We did not keep the lesson waiting.

## 0.1.1

The worm put on flesh. The field grew to fifty cells a side. Stains marked where the body had been. It was no longer a green arcade snake. It was beginning to be what it is.

## 0.1.0

The first coil. A green worm of squares upon a twenty-by-twenty field, four-way pad, red pellet, walls that kill. As our fathers knew Snake: humble, honest, and unashamed of being a rectangle.
